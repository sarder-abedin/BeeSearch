Grid-scale battery storage capacity worldwide grew by 42 percent in 2024, reaching a cumulative total of 190 gigawatt-hours according to industry trackers. Lithium-iron-phosphate chemistry now accounts for most new installations due to its lower cost and improved thermal stability compared to nickel-based cells.

Analysts attribute the surge to falling battery pack prices, which dropped below 115 dollars per kilowatt-hour for utility-scale systems, and to renewed government incentives for pairing storage with new solar and wind capacity.
